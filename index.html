Twenty years of evenings in Nacharam

For 20 years, Ghungroo has been the neighbourhood’s bar and restaurant. Locals come in after work, friends meet over a peg and a plate of Chicken 65, and families come back for the keema biryani.

The same hosts welcome you at the door, the kitchen cooks the recipes that made our name, and the bar pours the brands Nacharam asks for.
